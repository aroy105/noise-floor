NoiseFloor is a fused air and maritime GNSS interference detection tool that aims to estimate where jamming and spoofing originate in real time.

Adverseries, most prominently Russia, routinely employ GPS jamming and spoofing as a core part of their electronic warfare suite. Their objective is to blind the United States and its allies and partners, defend against drones and precision-guided weapons, protect leadership and sensitive sites, and pressure neighboring states through deniable disruption of civilian aviation and shipping. Actionable insights on what the adversary considers worth shielding can be derived by monitoring GNSS interference. 

This project seeks to detect this type of electronic warfare live, and as it relies only on open data, its findings can be shared openly.
